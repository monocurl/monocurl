Slicing a cone

Every circle, ellipse, parabola and hyperbola is a slice of one double cone. The cone's sides (its generators, like the white line) rise at 60°. Tilt the plane and compare its slope with theirs: flatter than a generator gives an ellipse, steeper cuts both halves and gives a hyperbola. Find the one tilt that gives a parabola. Drag to orbit, shift-drag to pan.
